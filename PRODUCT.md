# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Public visitors browse Ralph's work and personal interests. Ralph uses the private diary to track books, games, and reading activity.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Ralph's developer portfolio and a public shelf of selected books and games. The diary supports private tracking, with individual items published to the public shelf when Ralph chooses to share them.

## Brand Personality

Personal, cozy, creative.

## Anti-references

No specific visual anti-reference was named. Keep the library personal and cover-led, with compact, scannable metadata instead of a generic dashboard treatment.

## Design Principles

- Keep diary data private until an item is deliberately published.
- Show current status accurately for published items while honoring saved visibility preferences.
- Let book covers carry the visual weight and keep supporting details easy to scan.
- Preserve the existing dark and light themes and the library's pink accents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support. Give interactive controls clear labels and state.
